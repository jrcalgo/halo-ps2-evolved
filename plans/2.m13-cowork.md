## What this working tree already does

Host behavior is in `src/game/gun.c`, `arsenal.c`, `shot.c`, `vital.c`, and `grenade.c`. The play build is `src/game/play_ee.c` and `src/game/play_fp.c`, fed by `tools/play_weapons.py`.

`weapons.fp` stamp is `"7"`. After the `WFP1` weapon table and `RMP1` pose maps it has `AMO1` magazines and `PRJ1` primary projectiles, now including final velocity and air damage-range bounds for projectile deceleration. The pack is `build/play-objects/`. Reference packs under `data/retail` were not regenerated.

Magazines follow `weapon_new` and the retail tag: loaded is `min(rounds_total_initial, rounds_loaded_maximum)`, reserve is the rest. Reload length is the first-person reload-empty frame count, or 0 when that graph is absent. The tag’s reload seconds are not the timer. Flag bits in the record are waste, chamber-every-shot, partial ammunition, latch, magazine, and trigger. A wasting reload zeros the clip first. A latched trigger must be released. An emptying shot does not chamber. `rounds_recharged_per_second` is implemented and is 0 on every supplied tag.

Play HUD line is `NAME clip|reserve`. Weapons with `rounds_loaded_maximum == 0` show no count. F fires, R reloads, Q cycles all 23 paths.
