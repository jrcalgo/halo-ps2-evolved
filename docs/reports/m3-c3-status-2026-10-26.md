## Changes to the matrix since the 2026-10-24 ledger

- `render-v4-recompiler-cap48` became `render-v4-recompiler-cap64-lag2`: a 64-page texture cache cap with a 2-kick fence lag seen only by the texture cache (DMA chains and HUD owners keep the real fence). Without it, faster render paths mean no victim is ever in flight, so C3.7 `reuse_blocked_while_in_flight` has nothing to observe. The check itself is unchanged.
- Parallel matrix runs (default 4 jobs, slowest cases first). Closure directories are keyed by the workload-header hash and fence-lag value; before that a parallel run could link another case's player count.
- Captures for the successor cases are taken during live rendering, not after teardown.
