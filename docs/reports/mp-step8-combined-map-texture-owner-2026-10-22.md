# Step 8 diagnostic: co-resident multiplayer map and texture-owner test

## Result

The candidate multiplayer map topology was extended in a test-only ILP32 host fixture to load a map through CE's real map/tag/BSP path, retain its tag and BSP spans, and then load that same map's converter-produced `TEXS`, every emitted `LMn`, and scenario-selected `OTEX` section through halo-ps2's section loader into the immediately adjacent candidate texture-owner span. Existing CE collision, PVS, weather/wind, sound-environment, and lifecycle queries run after those texture loads and before that map unloads. The run passed all 13 stock multiplayer maps over two load/query/unload cycles (26 rows); campaign content is excluded.

This is a native host ILP32 diagnostic, not a PS2/EE execution, placement proof, texture-safety proof, or rendered-scene pass. It did not change production budgets, admission policy, CE gameplay, or map-service binders. The test keeps the candidate profile from the separate topology experiment; it does not establish that this profile should replace any production budget.

## Implementation/source evidence

- The candidate fixture injects the texture load after successful `scenario_load` and before its existing CE query/lifecycle sequence in `tests/hcu_mp_capacity_main.c:391` and `tests/hcu_mp_capacity_main.c:400`. The generated store in `tools/test_hcu_mp_candidate_topology.py` keeps tag, BSP, and texture slices contiguous and guarded; it is test-only.
- The bridge in `tests/mp_texture_candidate_owner.c:44` starts bounded arenas over the candidate texture and shared scratch slices, loads `TEXS`, visits `LM0` through `LM31`, and loads selected `OTEX` before returning to the CE fixture. It validates texture mips and lightmap views, confirms arena bounds, then verifies corrupted-CRC rollback (`tests/mp_texture_candidate_owner.c:70`, `:78`, `:94`, `:106`).
- The call is the actual `world_load_section_with_scratch` in `submodules/halo-ps2/engine/src/world/section.c:153`, which aligns decoded section output to 64 bytes and rolls back failed sections at `:199-243`. The decoded records are checked through the actual `tex_open` and `lm_open` implementations in `submodules/halo-ps2/engine/src/world/tex.c:28-71`.
- Converter asset derivation remains the prior isolated generator in `tools/test_halo_ps2_mp_texture_runtime.py:50-100`; it uses the retail CE scenario-selected model tag set, plus sky, to create the test's OTEX section payload.

## What remains unproven

This fixture deliberately does not establish target residency or actual EE allocation placement. It does not upload texture pages to GS, model renderer texture pins/eviction or simultaneous in-flight frames, use complete object packs (the wrappers contain OTEX only), prove custom sword/fuel-rod/gravity-rifle content is present, or inventory dynamic biped/animation/effect/HUD demand. The preceding static inventory is also not converted into a full match-time asset closure by this test. The reported rollback peak is a deliberate failure-path transient, and should not be mixed with normal retained demand. Step 8's capacity/topology and live-game renderer workload gates therefore remain open.
