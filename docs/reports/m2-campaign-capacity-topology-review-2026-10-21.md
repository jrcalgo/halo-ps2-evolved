# M2 campaign capacity topology review — next decisive route

**Disposition:** The current production representation cannot admit a campaign map by changing arena boundaries or streaming only the HP2 codec. CE publishes one contiguous, pointer-addressable tag image and one contiguous active BSP image; the d20 complete tag image plus its largest referenced BSP already breaks the entire budget-v2 committed ceiling after adding only fixed owners whose sizes are known. The strongest lossless *capacity experiment* is a typed, whole-record lease for `animation.data`, but existing evidence does not show it is sufficient and does not authorize implementation as a fit solution. First build a d20 owner/live-set and pointer-consumer experiment that can either validate that path or prove it insufficient, while keeping catalog admission closed. A Longest direct-destination vertical slice can validate M2 loader mechanics sooner, but it is a multiplayer map and cannot be cited as campaign capacity.

This is an independent, report-only audit of the current dirty worktree. It does not change runtime sources, tests, configuration, or milestone plans. `plans/5.hcu-remake.md` remains authoritative and retains campaign, AI, scripts, effects, and the 32 MiB PS2 target; `plans/7.hcu-remake-m2-m4.md` remains the execution plan. No N64 memory policy or feature cuts are proposed.

## Current ingress and what the new stream work buys

The current parent starts with a 2 MiB tag-cache default in [`hcu_main.c:23-25`](../../src/stream/hcu_main.c#L23), and [`hcu_memory_bind_parent_v2()`](../../src/stream/hcu_memory_bind.c#L41-L68) takes that one contiguous span from `current_bsp`, gives the remaining tail to the BSP provider, and sizes CE's claims bitmap from the configured tag+BSP spans. The production service still denies every non-UI leaf as `capacity-not-qualified` before opening its package ([`hcu_map_service.c:288-299`](../../src/stream/hcu_map_service.c#L288)). For accepted UI, preflight whole-loads HMAP, CTAG, CRNG and CBIX into `streaming` using `world_load_section_with_scratch()` ([`hcu_map_service.c:57-80,329-357`](../../src/stream/hcu_map_service.c#L57)); the BSP visitor added in current source applies only to the later exact CBSP read ([`hcu_map_service.c:98-119,171-180`](../../src/stream/hcu_map_service.c#L98)). Thus the current production path has not acquired a streamed CTAG or campaign admission path.

Bounded HP2 decoding is valuable because it removes a duplicate encoded-section staging allocation. It does not alter the final CE tag or BSP bytes required by raw-pointer consumers. Current direct-destination CBSP logic can be qualified against the Longest package, whose final CTAG+B000 images fit arithmetically in the unchanged `current_bsp` arena under the candidate 4,477,120/1,814,336 split. That is the closest loader vertical slice, but Longest is not a campaign map, and its current package integration has no full CE campaign state/AI/encounter/render/audio high-water proof. The Longest reconciliation report explicitly records that its direct BSP visitor has not yet been composed through the whole retail service and guest lifecycle; its CTAG whole-section strategy still cannot fit the streaming arena ([`m2-longest-streamed-live-set-reconciliation-2026-10-21.md`](m2-longest-streamed-live-set-reconciliation-2026-10-21.md)).

## The campaign lower bound is not an arena-partition problem

The matching campaign corpus reports compact CE tag images from 6,267,540 B to 11,781,424 B. Pairing each map with its largest complete referenced raw BSP (required to support all scenario BSP references one-at-a-time) gives 10,310,876 B for the smallest `d40 + d40_space` case and 18,564,628 B for `d20 + d20_hangar`; both values omit all conversion/loading scratch and unrelated owners. The aligned d20 spans are tag 10,047,008 B + BSP 8,517,632 B = 18,564,640 B. Its CE claims bitmap, computed from span capacity by the current PS2 validator, is another 2,320,580 B ([`tag_validate.c:244-283`](../../submodules/halo-ce-universal/port/linux/game/tag_validate.c#L244)).

The lower bound adds only current known fixed owners: system/kernel 1,572,864 B, ELF LOAD bytes 2,086,532 B, ELF `.bss` 2,114,588 B, CE game-state 3,428,352 B, the aligned tag+BSP pair 18,564,640 B, and claims 2,320,580 B. Total: **30,087,556 B**, already **2,300,292 B above** the 27,787,264-B committed ceiling. This excludes every byte for texture/lightmaps, dynamic objects and campaign actors, effects, scripts, DMA, streaming, checkpoint, audio, frame scratch, alignment, and map-transition overlap. These are independently owned EE bytes; GS/IOP/SPU2 capacity cannot back CE pointers ([`plans/5.hcu-remake.md:259-267`](../../plans/5.hcu-remake.md#L259)). Consequently, no re-cut of the existing region names can make this *whole-resident uncompressed representation* fit while preserving the reserve. This does not disprove a new representation that changes pointer consumers or measured phase sharing.

At the other extreme, even the hypothetical removal of **all** d20 `animation.data` bytes, with zero cache/provider/staging cost, leaves just 2,751,784 B for every omitted owner. For a10, the corresponding residual is only 2,404,132 B. The 2,621,440-B dynamic simulation reservation alone is larger, though its true high-water and overlap with other owners have not been measured. Do not assert either that animation leasing is sufficient or that summing all nominal arena capacities proves failure: both require actual lifetime/high-water evidence. The key implication is that an animation-only change is unlikely to close the full campaign budget; a d20 instrumentation experiment must quantify all remaining removable candidate bytes and actual required live owners before any new pool sizes are proposed. These bounds and map rows are derived in [`m2-capacity-viable-design-review-2026-10-21.md:37-75`](m2-capacity-viable-design-review-2026-10-21.md#L37) and [`m2-2026-10-21-budget-repartition-review.md:48-91`](m2-2026-10-21-budget-repartition-review.md#L48).

## CE pointer topology that blocks transparent paging

1. **Tag blocks and tag data are raw pointers after fixup.** `struct tag_block.address` and `struct tag_data.address` are address fields; `tag_block_get_element_with_size()` and `tag_data_get_pointer()` return raw arithmetic based on them ([`tag_groups.h:64-145`](../../submodules/halo-ce-universal/source/tag_files/tag_groups.h#L64), [`tag_groups.c:91-145`](../../submodules/halo-ce-universal/source/tag_files/tag_groups.c#L91)). Numerous call sites dereference `.address` directly, so adding I/O only inside `tag_data_get_pointer()` cannot transparently page all fields.
2. **The cache publishes a complete, stable image.** CE reads the complete `header->tag_data_size` into the bound cache, verifies the header and schema graph, then sets `tag_header`, `global_tag_instances`, count, and loaded state ([`cache_files.c:1258-1332`](../../submodules/halo-ce-universal/source/cache/cache_files.c#L1258)). Tag datum base addresses and nested blocks/data remain meaningful only while the underlying image stays fixed. CE scenario unload clears those globals before the parent releases ownership, which is the safe destructive-reload ordering ([`scenario.c:370-412`](../../submodules/halo-ce-universal/source/scenario/scenario.c#L370); tag-cache unpublication in [`cache_files.c:640-667`](../../submodules/halo-ce-universal/source/cache/cache_files.c#L640)).
3. **The active BSP has a similar complete-image contract.** CE requests a BSP into its configured fixed base, performs structure/BSP validation and relocation, then publishes `global_collision_bsp`/structure state ([`scenario.c:1120-1192`](../../submodules/halo-ce-universal/source/scenario/scenario.c#L1120); PS2 exact-destination checks in [`ps2_cache.c:490-628`](../../submodules/halo-ce-universal/port/ps2/src/ps2_cache.c#L490)). Only one active BSP at a time is a useful bound; BSP3D collision, surfaces/material links, cluster PVS and sound data remain behaviorally required. Consumer audit finds no whole field family safe to omit today ([`m2-step7-bsp-consumer-disposition.md:23-79`](m2-step7-bsp-consumer-disposition.md#L23)).
4. **Disk and other PS2 stores are not transparent address backing.** FileXio copies bounded reads into caller-owned EE buffers; DVD/OPL, IOP RAM, GS eDRAM and SPU2 RAM cannot be directly dereferenced as EE tag pointers. A cache/paging API must return an EE lease that cannot be evicted until all readers release it; async tick reads and DMA readers need explicit lifetime fences.

The best-quantified candidate is `animation.data`: it accounts for 2.55–4.97 MiB/map in the audited corpus, with a largest observed record of 167,560 B. It is still not a drop-in streaming field. The schema validator directly reads compressed bytes before publication ([`tag_schema_models.c:862-927`](../../submodules/halo-ce-universal/port/linux/game/tag_schema_models.c#L862)); ordinary frame acquisition and three compressed rotation/translation/scale helpers read tag data at different paths ([`model_animations.c:2152-2188`](../../submodules/halo-ce-universal/source/models/model_animations.c#L2152), [`:2296-2325`](../../submodules/halo-ce-universal/source/models/model_animations.c#L2296), [`:2431-2459`](../../submodules/halo-ce-universal/source/models/model_animations.c#L2431)); four-frame aiming interpolation holds four frame pointers simultaneously ([`model_animations.c:1876-1888`](../../submodules/halo-ce-universal/source/models/model_animations.c#L1876)). The source audit finds no persistent frame pointers in `animation_state`, which makes a *whole-record scoped lease* credible for experimentation, not yet proven safe. `frame_info` and `default_data` are distinct fields and remain resident in that narrow experiment.

## Most viable lossless route and decisive experiment

There is no evidence-backed campaign-fit design ready to implement. The lowest-risk experiment is to measure and prototype a **lossless whole-record `animation.data` lease on d20**, not a frame cache that may evict pointers, and not an arena-size change:

1. Freeze one matching tuple: retail d20 map/source hash, converter and CTAG sidecar hashes, schema ELF, HCU/parent/halo-ps2 dirty file hashes, toolchain/flags, production closure/ELF, budget, and memory map. Re-run d20 schema/range extraction from that tuple; do not join the older `2fba…` reports to a different source/image without regeneration.
2. Add diagnostic access tracing at schema validation, `animation_get_frame_data()`, each compressed keyframe reader, and all other direct data-address consumers discovered by a source-aware inventory. Record map/tag/graph/animation identity, field, pointer range, begin/end, concurrent pins, bytes touched, cache miss bytes/seeks, failure, and EE cycles. Exercise actual d20 hangar plus all authored encounters, actor/object/first-person poses and the four-pointer interpolation path. This determines whether the record access is bounded and whether the data set is mostly cold; payload totals alone do not size the cache.
3. Prototype a stable per-record source descriptor and explicit acquire/release lease that returns the exact logical byte sequence at a contiguous EE address. Make it visible to schema validation before graph publication, route *all* direct consumers, preserve compressed/uncompressed acceptance/correction and pose results against the existing resident oracle, handle nested acquisition, and forbid map unload/eviction with live leases. First run under deliberately undersized caches and injected media failures to establish deterministic failure behavior.
4. In the same d20 vertical fixture, measure the complete simultaneous EE owner set and phase overlap—not just tag savings—including exact tag/BSP/claims spans, CE fixed game state, dynamics (actors/AI/scripts/effects/projectiles), BSP collision and surface/material queries, renderer source/pack staging and textures, DMA, sound/audio staging, streaming/HP2 scratch, snapshots, allocator metadata, and failed/successful transitions. Make interval/canary evidence prove every EE byte has one owner. Keep the 5.5 MiB reserve unchanged.
5. **Decision gate:** if the measured lossless lease cache plus the remaining owners fits with margin in v2 and survives collision/material/PVS/sound/AI/script/render and unload/retry parity, then design a generalized map capacity contract and PCSX2 d20 load. If even the optimistic bound plus actual measured non-animation owners exceeds the committed ceiling, stop animation-only work and audit a second typed candidate (BSP render-data replacement) only through its complete CE consumer/query parity; if no set of lossless typed changes fits, record the campaign map as currently infeasible under v2 and present a measured design alternative without silently dropping scope or reserve. Do not open catalog admission based on a host-only lease prototype.

This route is the strongest *decisive experiment*, not a claim it will succeed. Existing bound says full `animation.data` removal alone cannot plausibly buy the whole missing budget, but only measured high-water and source-consumer evidence can close the question. The `d20_hangar` render mapping has 49,859 matched source surfaces, but it also has quantization/UV outliers; its replacement pack grows the relevant section by 565,376 B before an additional 199,436-B source mapping, and CE lighting/surface consumers remain. It is therefore a second experimental target, not a current memory credit ([`m2-capacity-viable-design-review-2026-10-21.md:78-83`](m2-capacity-viable-design-review-2026-10-21.md#L78), [`m2-d20-hangar-render-mapping-audit.md:17-48`](m2-d20-hangar-render-mapping-audit.md#L17)).

## Halo-64 comparison (method only)

Halo-64 demonstrates the method of adding a platform-specific accessor: CE's `animation_get_frame_data()` branches to `n64_animation_frame()` for specially marked animation records ([`model_animation_definitions.c:498-523`](../../submodules/halo-64/source/models/model_animation_definitions.c#L498)), and N64 stores animation frames in a 16 × 4 KiB cartridge-backed cache ([`n64_anim.c:22-24,108-177`](../../submodules/halo-64/port/n64/src/n64_anim.c#L22)). Its own comments explain the cache is boot-reserved to avoid later map allocations becoming fragmented ([`n64_anim.c:48-63`](../../submodules/halo-64/port/n64/src/n64_anim.c#L48)). This validates a source-level seam as a useful experiment, not the correctness of this topology: Halo-64's cartridge/DMA, N64 memory, cache timing, 8 MiB constraints, and its own CE cuts do not translate to PS2 DVD/OPL plus 32 MiB EE. More importantly, HCU has direct compressed readers and a four-pointer interpolation consumer that any PS2 lease must preserve. Do not copy its chunk size, LRU/eviction behavior, ABI, feature reductions, or capacity.

## Recommended acceptance sequence and current disposition

1. Keep M2 catalog admission restricted to `ui`; current source rejects every non-UI map before opening the package.
2. Finish the composed Longest B000 visitor and direct-destination transaction tests as a bounded *loader mechanic* test; only after a full CE scenario lifecycle and exact PCSX2 guest run, describe Longest as a loaded multiplayer map. This does not satisfy campaign capacity.
3. Run the d20 lease/live-set experiment above and publish its source-specific owner ledger, hashes, query/pose parity, errors, and timing. A cache size must be an output of measurement, not an initial assumption.
4. If the experiment finds a credible budget, materialize exact d20 tag/BSP data through the production CE validation/scenario paths, test all 1-at-a-time referenced BSPs, campaign encounter AI/script/effect state, material/surface/collision/PVS/sound queries, resource reads, map unload/reload and fault retry. Run the exact frozen ELF in PCSX2 interpreter and recompiler; compare state/high-water and capture the framebuffer. A black viewport still does not prove a rendered scene.
5. Bind actual target memory, claims, stack/kernel ranges and owner high-water to the same artifact before M2 exit. Keep the reserve intact and measure physical PS2 timing/storage separately; PCSX2 cannot establish optical/OPL media latency or hardware EE peak.

**Conclusion:** Longest is the most promising first *actual gameplay-map* loader exercise; d20 is the decisive *campaign capacity* experiment. Neither is currently admitted or proven. A streamed HP2 reader solves transient duplicate staging only. To fit campaign under v2 without scope loss, change and prove a CE data-access/lifetime contract (and likely more than one data family), or gather evidence for safe, lifetimed phase reuse. Do not claim campaign M2 completion, mark the milestone complete, change budget rows, or spend reserve based on this report.
