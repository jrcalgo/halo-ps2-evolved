# M2 Step 6 current evidence review — 2026-10-21

## Disposition

**Step 6 is substantially complete at the host semantic/integration layer, but should remain partial as an M2 acceptance step.** Current reports and fixtures demonstrate the real CE BSP switch/load/unload logic working with the parent package mount, halo-ps2 HP2 section reader, and HCU PS2 cache/CBSP materializer on package-derived `a30`/`a50` inputs. Separate D20 host lifecycle work adds `scenario_load()`/`scenario_unload()`, all five D20 BSP states, and B001–B004 integrity-failure restoration/retry. This is materially stronger than the old Step 6 reports that described a CE model or fake loader.

The unclosed gate is now primarily **target/capacity evidence**, not lack of a host test of CE's switch body. The campaign-capacity audit says no campaign CTAG plus even its smallest referenced BSP fits the current single `current_bsp` span; its most favorable lower bound is 10,310,880 bytes versus 6,291,456 bytes available. A PCSX2 switch test on a gameplay map cannot be run lawfully with current bindings. Do not mark Step 6 complete by substituting the UI map or overprovisioned host arrays for a capacity-qualified gameplay map.

## Conclusion and next gate

The initial Step 6 investigation's major recommendation—execute real CE switch/load/unload against the production PS2/cache/parent section path and package-derived BSPs—has been achieved for host ILP32. This removes the historical “model only” limitation. Source review also confirms the PS2 normalization of the Xbox renderer buffer handles is narrowly pre-validation and distinct from stripping CE collision/PVS/sound data; it does not weaken CE schema/verifier paths ([`plans/7.hcu-remake-m2-m4.md:574`](../../plans/7.hcu-remake-m2-m4.md#L574)).

Do not mark Step 6 wholly complete yet. The initial review's host rerun and explicit relocation-error caveats are superseded by the reassessment below. For final milestone acceptance, keep campaign-map admission closed while Step 8's capacity blocker stands; then, only after a lawful production-sized map fits, run exact guest success, candidate-failure→rollback, rollback-failure→safe-null/menu, retry, and unload in both PCSX2 interpreter and recompiler with physical high-water and exact ELF/package hashes. The M2 Step 9 audit is consistent: extensive host coverage exists, but it does not establish a target-backed campaign transaction or the full cross-owner fault matrix ([`m2-step9-transaction-coverage-audit-2026-10-21.md`](m2-step9-transaction-coverage-audit-2026-10-21.md)).
