## Changes in this lane

- `tools/c2_budget_donor_slack.py` (new): reads the owner audit JSON and the budget, lists each donor with its source,
  applies a 10% (min 64 KiB) margin, counts only complete multiplayer measurements as safe, and reports the shortfall.
- `tests/test_hcu_c2_budget_donors.py` (new): 5 tests. Incomplete measurements are never safe; the overflow is not
  covered; a complete donor is counted after the margin; ungated diagnostics are not counted as donors; the live audit
