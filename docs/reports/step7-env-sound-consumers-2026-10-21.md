# Step 7 report: multiplayer BSP environment and sound consumers

Date: 2026-10-21  
Scope: report-only source/test audit for Step 7 of [`plans/7.hcu-remake-m2-m4.md`](../../plans/7.hcu-remake-m2-m4.md). The product scope is the 13 multiplayer maps (`beavercreek`, `bloodgulch`, `boardingaction`, `carousel`, `chillout`, `damnation`, `hangemhigh`, `longest`, `prisoner`, `putput`, `ratrace`, `sidewinder`, `wizard`) plus the separate UI bootstrap; campaign missions/maps are excluded. The catalog and scope list are recorded at [`src/stream/hcu_map_catalog.c:135-261`](../../src/stream/hcu_map_catalog.c#L135) and [`plans/7.hcu-remake-m2-m4.md:246`](../../plans/7.hcu-remake-m2-m4.md#L246).

## Finding

The Step 7 BSP preservation work does establish that source BSP bytes are retained through conversion and that CE can consume selected relocated fog/water and sound fields. It does **not** establish multiplayer-map parity for the environment/audio families: the current end-to-end consumer fixture loads only `a30` and `a50` (both campaign maps, now out of product scope), the standalone wind fixture is pinned to `a30`, and the environment queries exercise only selected candidate clusters/BSPs rather than all relevant state transitions. There is no sound playback/SPU2 test here. In addition, `scenario_fog_at_point()` currently returns constant `0.0f` despite real AI perception callers, which is an explicit unresolved behavior defect rather than merely a missing test.

Therefore these findings keep Step 7 open. They should be used as the focused next implementation/test slice; they do not justify deleting or stripping any BSP field family.

## CE data and consumer map

The BSP schema places these data in independent, semantically consumed blocks. `structure_cluster` carries fog reference, background sound palette index, sound-environment palette index, and weather palette index at offsets `+0x02`, `+0x04`, `+0x06`, and `+0x08` respectively ([`structure_bsp_definitions.h:89-110`](../../submodules/halo-ce-universal/source/structures/structure_bsp_definitions.h#L89)). `structure_bsp` owns fog planes/regions/palette, weather palette/polyhedra, background-sound and sound-environment palettes, and encoded cluster-pair sound data ([`structure_bsp_definitions.h:197-219`](../../submodules/halo-ce-universal/source/structures/structure_bsp_definitions.h#L197)). Palette/plane records have asserted retail strides: background sound `0x74`, sound environment `0x50`, fog plane `0x20`, fog region `0x28`, fog palette `0x88` ([`structure_bsp_definitions.h:112-176`](../../submodules/halo-ce-universal/source/structures/structure_bsp_definitions.h#L112)). These fields and blocks must remain in the lossless preserved BSP unless later source-backed proof shows a field is unused across the required multiplayer path.

### Fog, water, and location

- `scenario_get_fog_region_index()` resolves a cluster's direct fog region or a high-bit fog-plane reference, classifies a point against that plane and fog `plane_distance`, and bounds-checks the referenced plane/region ([`scenario.c:632-692`](../../submodules/halo-ce-universal/source/scenario/scenario.c#L632)).
- `scenario_location_underwater()` resolves fog-plane → region → fog tag, reads the water flag, prefers region weather over cluster weather, and validates the selected weather index ([`scenario.c:694-741`](../../submodules/halo-ce-universal/source/scenario/scenario.c#L694)). `scenario_location_water_depth()` computes signed plane-relative depth only for a water fog definition; otherwise it returns `REAL_MIN` ([`scenario.c:743-801`](../../submodules/halo-ce-universal/source/scenario/scenario.c#L743)).
- `scenario_fog_region_get_fog_index()` validates both region and palette indexes before returning the fog datum ([`scenario.c:1073-1098`](../../submodules/halo-ce-universal/source/scenario/scenario.c#L1073)). `scenario_get_atmospheric_fog()` selects sky indoor/outdoor fog, updates per-local-player interpolation state, and fills CPU render-fog fields ([`scenario.c:1469-1634`](../../submodules/halo-ce-universal/source/scenario/scenario.c#L1469)).
- Critical gap: `scenario_fog_at_point()` is a constant-zero stub ([`scenario.c:803-809`](../../submodules/halo-ce-universal/source/scenario/scenario.c#L803)), while CE calls it from AI visibility/perception code ([`ai.c:2363`](../../submodules/halo-ce-universal/source/ai/ai.c#L2363), [`actor_perception.c:2484`](../../submodules/halo-ce-universal/source/ai/actor_perception.c#L2484)). Since multiplayer bots are in scope, that stub can remove fog-based AI perception attenuation even though campaign is out of scope. It needs an implementation and multiplayer fixture, not classification as a renderer-only callback.
- **Implementation disposition (2026-10-21): blocked on an authoritative behavior oracle.** The pinned Halo-64 `scenario_fog_at_point()` is the same zero-return stub ([`scenario.c:780-786`](../../submodules/halo-64/source/scenario/scenario.c#L780)), so it cannot supply the missing CE formula. CE callsites establish thresholds/use only: LOS branches at 0.8/0.6 ([`ai.c:2363-2372`](../../submodules/halo-ce-universal/source/ai/ai.c#L2363)) and perception attenuation above 0.8 / above 0.2 ([`actor_perception.c:2484-2503`](../../submodules/halo-ce-universal/source/ai/actor_perception.c#L2484)). The exposed fog fields include maximum density/distance/depth and plane distance in the tag schema ([`tag_schema_render.c:911-929`](../../submodules/halo-ce-universal/port/linux/game/tag_schema_render.c#L911)), but no local source defines how to combine them along a viewer-to-target segment across fog/cluster boundaries. Do not replace the stub with a plausible formula: that would invent bot behavior. To close this subcheck, recover the original CE function body/disassembly or capture controlled retail CE outputs for multiplayer scenarios spanning no-fog/direct-fog/plane references, plane-side and region crossings, and varied density/distance. Until then, mark fog-based AI attenuation as a known parity blocker.
- Existing integration fixture has a good targeted fog-plane oracle: it independently reads raw Xbox values and checks plane, region, fog datum, region-vs-cluster weather, both sides of a plane, underwater flag, depth, publication identity, and collision-depth stability ([`hcu_scenario_bsp_switch_main.c:1294-1402`](../../tests/hcu_scenario_bsp_switch_main.c#L1294)). However the Python runner builds only `a30` and `a50` packages ([`test_hcu_scenario_bsp_switch.py:1478-1541`](../../tests/test_hcu_scenario_bsp_switch.py#L1478)); it does not cover any of the 13 required multiplayer maps.
- The same transaction calls `scenario_location_underwater()` once on the first `fog_reference == NONE` cluster per BSP ([`hcu_scenario_bsp_switch_main.c:1603-1635`](../../tests/hcu_scenario_bsp_switch_main.c#L1603)). It verifies the dry result and pointer/depth stability, but does not test invalid fog-region/palette/plane indexes, water/non-water transitions across cluster boundaries, fallback weather selection in all cases, or output immutability on malformed fields.
- There are no current direct tests for `scenario_get_atmospheric_fog()` or the fog attenuation function. Parent `tests/test_location.py` exercises the separate PS2 location pack/read path, not these CE APIs; it is not evidence of CE environment behavior.

### Weather and wind

- CE's `wind_update()` advances a time counter, iterates weather palette rows, updates deterministic seeded velocity/angular variation, consumes each weather row's wind tag/direction/magnitude, and stores a per-entry runtime wind state ([`wind.c:184-271`](../../submodules/halo-ce-universal/source/scenario/wind.c#L184)). The current limit caps entries at the fixed 32 `wind_states` slots ([`wind.c:116-126`](../../submodules/halo-ce-universal/source/scenario/wind.c#L116)); the inventory must verify all 13 map palette counts remain within this limit.
- `scenario_get_current()` resolves cluster weather and fog-region weather (with force-water/no-water behavior), then returns the wind/current sample; `scenario_get_wind()` and `scenario_get_water_current()` select distinct flag paths ([`wind.c:348-445`](../../submodules/halo-ce-universal/source/scenario/wind.c#L348)). Those APIs can affect gameplay, effects, and bots, not just drawing.
- The current raw retail wind test independently reads the `a30` source map and wind-tag definitions, executes real CE `wind_initialize_for_new_map()`, `wind_update()`, and `scenario_get_wind()`, compares normal/simple/damped vectors, and checks repeat stability and the missing-wind zero path ([`test_hcu_wind_consumer.py:44-122`](../../tests/test_hcu_wind_consumer.py#L44), [`test_hcu_wind_consumer.py:237-345`](../../tests/test_hcu_wind_consumer.py#L237)). It is a meaningful algorithm oracle but is explicitly hard-coded to `a30` and cluster 33 ([`test_hcu_wind_consumer.py:238-249`](../../tests/test_hcu_wind_consumer.py#L238)), so it proves no multiplayer-map weather/wind data or transitions.
- BSP weather palette also points to weather-particle-system tags. CE initializes and owns particle data and has per-local-player systems ([`weather_particle_systems.c:326-371`](../../submodules/halo-ce-universal/source/effects/weather_particle_systems.c#L326)); `weather_particle_system_render()` calls `weather_particle_system_update()` before traversing weather polyhedra and emission ([`weather_particle_systems.c:561-635`](../../submodules/halo-ce-universal/source/effects/weather_particle_systems.c#L561)). Thus this is stateful work embedded in a render-named call; a PS2 no-op renderer must not accidentally discard required update, pool, or weather selection behavior. There is no current package-backed multiplayer test for weather-particle initialization, update/capacity, polyhedra selection, or bounded failure.

### Sound environment, background loops, and acoustic table

- `scenario_get_sound_environment()` iterates all four local-player slots, samples each active camera's cluster/fog, selects the highest-priority sound environment across fog and cluster palettes, chooses associated background sound, tracks the underwater boundary, and smoothly approaches the chosen environment ([`scenario.c:1310-1455`](../../submodules/halo-ce-universal/source/scenario/scenario.c#L1310)). This matters directly to four-player split-screen: the current transaction test creates only one active local player and initializes the selector as non-underwater ([`hcu_scenario_bsp_switch_main.c:1640-1709`](../../tests/hcu_scenario_bsp_switch_main.c#L1640)). It does not test priority conflicts, multiple players in different clusters/environments, underwater boundary transitions, default/no-entry selection, or the immediate copy vs smoothing step.
- Existing package transaction oracle independently derives candidate palette/environment/background values from the raw Xbox BSP and requires CE's selected palette/tag/background tuple to match ([`test_hcu_scenario_bsp_switch.py:1669-1680`](../../tests/test_hcu_scenario_bsp_switch.py#L1669), [`test_hcu_scenario_bsp_switch.py:1721-1746`](../../tests/test_hcu_scenario_bsp_switch.py#L1721)). But the fixture scans until the first fog-free cluster with a valid environment palette and then breaks; it tests only one qualifying cluster per BSP, and the outer fixture remains limited to `a30`/`a50` ([`hcu_scenario_bsp_switch_main.c:1651-1708`](../../tests/hcu_scenario_bsp_switch_main.c#L1651), [`test_hcu_scenario_bsp_switch.py:1502-1508`](../../tests/test_hcu_scenario_bsp_switch.py#L1502)). It initializes `crossed_water_boundary=TRUE` but expects `FALSE` because the selected cluster is dry and the fixture resets `sound_environment_initialized=FALSE`; wet transition behavior is not covered.
- `game_sound_update()` consumes the selected background loop and passes the environment to `sound_manager_set_sound_environment()` before calculating combined PAS and starting/stopping/replacing background-loop sounds ([`game_sound.c:820-860`](../../submodules/halo-ce-universal/source/sound/game_sound.c#L820)). The actual platform listener update checks underwater state per active player, triggers entry/exit splash sounds, updates listener position/velocity, and forwards the selected environment to the platform backend ([`sound_manager.c:3820-3927`](../../submodules/halo-ce-universal/source/sound/sound_manager.c#L3820)). The current scenario fixture does not call this full update, does not test sound-cache decoding/streaming or sound requests, and cannot prove audible SPU2 playback.
- CE's encoded sound-cluster data is a packed one-byte-per-unordered-pair matrix. The accessor canonicalizes pair order and returns 0 for same-cluster queries or max/unreachable for invalid/out-of-range pairs ([`structure_bsp_definitions.c:207-267`](../../submodules/halo-ce-universal/source/structures/structure_bsp_definitions.c#L207)). The broad package transaction does check pair accessors, symmetry, same-cluster behavior, and source-byte preservation, and its raw source oracle reconstructs listener PAS rows ([`test_hcu_scenario_bsp_switch.py:341-448`](../../tests/test_hcu_scenario_bsp_switch.py#L341), [`hcu_scenario_bsp_switch_main.c:1404-1560`](../../tests/hcu_scenario_bsp_switch_main.c#L1404)). It also exercises CE `compute_combined_pas()` and compares all BSPs in its input maps ([`test_hcu_scenario_bsp_switch.py:1747-1766`](../../tests/test_hcu_scenario_bsp_switch.py#L1747)). These are useful PAS/occlusion inputs, but do not cover `compute_sound_obstruction()` scenarios such as same cluster, adjacent/occluded, encoded unreachable, distance cutoff, multiple listeners, cache refresh/time cadence, or dynamic-object occlusion; the exact Step 7 status note already calls this limitation out ([`plans/7.hcu-remake-m2-m4.md:603`](../../plans/7.hcu-remake-m2-m4.md#L603)).
- The test-only game-sound bridge directly compiles CE `game_sound.c`, provides camera fixtures, and can spy on collision calls for sound obstruction ([`hcu_game_sound_host32.c:1-105`](../../tests/hcu_game_sound_host32.c#L1)), but its current callers primarily exercise selected early-return/no-collision branches. No test invokes full sound playback through the HCU map transaction or the PS2/SPU2 side.

## Fastest valid implementation and test order

This is an integration sequence, not permission to replace CE calculations with fixture-only behavior:

1. **Correct the concrete fog behavior first.** Implement `scenario_fog_at_point()` according to the CE source/reference semantics (including viewer/target location handling, cluster/plane transitions, and distance/attenuation), then add a host ILP32 fixture that links the real CE implementation. Build independent expected values from retail tag/BSP bytes, test points on both sides of a fog boundary and non-fog/default behavior, and assert AI perception callsites observe the value. Keep collision/pointer globals stable during the query.
2. **Parameterize the package oracle over the 13 MP package manifest.** Retain raw Xbox BSP/tag as the independent oracle and use each package's real relocation path. For each MP BSP, record counts and index validity for fog planes/regions/palettes, weather palette/polyhedra, sound palettes and sound-pair matrix before selecting cases. Use a generated case table rather than first-match-only loops, so every distinct branch is covered while avoiding redundant identical clusters.
3. **Extend the existing fixture rather than fork it.** `test_hcu_scenario_bsp_switch.py` already links real `scenario.c`, real `sound_environment_definitions.c`, production PS2 cache/CTAG/CBSP adapters and real BSP switching. Add multipayer observer fixtures (0–3/ four slots as CE defines), controlled camera clusters and deterministic order tests. Assert priority winner, fog-over-cluster precedence, background loop index, immediate water boundary transition, then per-field smoothing bounds across repeated calls. Preserve existing map-switch/global rollback invariants.
4. **Exercise live sound logic end-to-end through request capture.** Drive `game_sound_update()` and `sound_manager_update()` with package-backed map state and intercept only the platform sound request boundary. Assert chosen environment/background events, PAS/obstruction per source/listener, no request for unreachable/far branches where CE forbids it, stable cache refresh, and exactly-once underwater entry/exit event. The fake may capture output requests; it must not replace CE selector/obstruction code.
5. **Qualify weather/wind and render scheduling.** Generalize the raw wind oracle to MP palette rows; test update output once per simulation tick under render schedules 0/1/3, not by repeatedly calling a render function that advances particle state. For particle systems, test counts/alloc failure and polyhedra bounds from actual MP tags. Feed only resulting CPU weather/fog state to the PS2 adapter; Halo-64 is useful only for the distinction between simulation/presentation adapters, not to inherit N64's disabled-render/weather behavior ([`submodules/halo-64/port/n64/NOTES.md:33-55`](../../submodules/halo-64/port/n64/NOTES.md#L33)).
6. **Promote to target-sized evidence only after Step 8 capacity is qualified.** Use the exact production arena split and catalog load path; record map/package/source hashes, active map/BSP ownership, scratch and weather/effect high-water, 1–4 local player count, and target/PCSX2 interpreter+recompiler result. Do not use the current `a30`/`a50` host fixture as target evidence.

## Recommended Step 7 exit checks from this audit

- Every retained BSP environment/acoustic field family is enumerated and mapped to one of: live CE gameplay/bot behavior, sound-selection/playback input, PS2 presentation, or proven irrelevant. No environment family is stripped based solely on the renderer being incomplete.
- All 13 MP maps have at least structural extents/index bounds for the relevant blocks, and branch-focused package-backed CE tests cover each distinct fog/water/weather/sound behavior actually present in those maps. Maps with no such tags still test defined default behavior.
- `scenario_fog_at_point()` is no longer a zero stub and has raw-source-backed perception assertions for MP bot scenarios.
- Four-local-player listener selection and underwater transitions are verified, and sound requests reach the PS2 adapter. An audible SPU2 result may be recorded as platform acceptance separately, but must not be conflated with the CE selector test.
- Weather/wind/particle simulation advances at the intended tick cadence and is invariant to extra renders; bounded pool/arena failure leaves map globals and ownership valid.
- Step 8 still owns production capacity/high-water and target-sized residency. Passing these consumer checks does not itself close Step 8 or authorize the unproven compacted Sidewinder projection.

## Evidence limitations

This report inspected source and current fixture definitions; it did not modify implementation or execute the tests. The report's source findings should be corroborated against raw source/package hashes when the multiplayer fixture runs. Halo-64 is cited only as an architectural comparator for separating stateful adapters from drawing. REA is not applicable to this source/test audit; no PS2 binary reverse-engineering question was needed.
