## Reserve question: 0x1a00000 vs 0x1a80000

- 27,787,264 B (the committed budget) = `0x1a80000` exactly.
- 27,787,264 + 5,767,168 (the reserve) = `0x2000000` (32 MiB). So the reserve begins at `0x1a80000`.
- The guest prints `reserve=0x1a00000 reserve_bytes=5767168` (`src/stream/hcu_main.c:505` and `:649` hardcode `0x1a00000`).
  Its own `reserve_bytes` agrees with `0x1a80000`, so the printed start is off by `0x80000` (512 KiB).
- Measured ELF LOAD highest byte (`readelf -lW`, end of `memsz`), lifecycle ELF (fresh): `4,730,636` (`0x4846CC`);
  the Oct 21 ELF: `4,984,716` (`0x4C0F8C`); the Oct 22 4-player ELF: `5,215,380` (`0x4F9494`).
  All three are below both `0x1a00000` (27,262,976) and `0x1a80000` (27,787,264). No ELF reaches the reserve.
- Fix needed (not in this lane's ownership): derive the printed reserve from `GUEST_RESERVE_START`, not the
  literal `0x1a00000`, in `src/stream/hcu_main.c`.
