  `.MIPS.abiflags`) so `code_and_constant_bytes` is shown as its parts; `unowned_non_diagnostic_bss_bytes`, which

  `top_bss_symbols`, `top_data_symbols` and `top_text_symbols` with the largest `--top` (default 30) symbols and their

  that the top tables are sorted and bounded. The ELF checks run only when the fresh ELF exists.

ai_debug gate from the r3 lane is already in the measured ELF (`ai_debug` is 236,356 B, not 547,628 B).

### Why no shrink was implemented

- Every large unowned item is either CE state with no measured PS2 size (the grenade, collision, netcode and vehicle
  tables) or is referenced by code outside this lane. Lowering a cap without a measured peak risks the gameplay data
  it protects.
- The diagnostic items total about 26 KB outside `ai_debug`. That is too small to close the overflow.
- The libc printf and scanf families are linked for the port's own use. Removing them needs a call-graph check and a
  port change, not an edit to the CE tables.

## (4) Remaining overflow and candidate revision

- Exact overflow on this ELF: **979,064 B**.
- The only measured donor is `streaming` slack of 587,336 B (r3 report). It is 391,728 B short of the overflow, so
  it cannot cover this need alone.
- `resident_gameplay` usage beyond its owned 10,240 B is unmeasured (C2.6), so it is not a donor.
- No budget revision is proposed. The committed sum equals the sum of all regions (27,787,264 B), so any `elf_static`
  growth must come from another region's measured slack. The reserve (5,767,168 B) is untouched.
