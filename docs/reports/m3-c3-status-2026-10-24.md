## Current-source guest failure

The retry artifact tuple and hashes are recorded in the JSON ledger. The CE log shows Slayer selected, `game-engine-running=1`, `map-loaded=1`, one connected controller, and the workload action pattern armed. The first render reports four views and one begin/submission; DMA telemetry reports one emit failure. About 10 ms later the emulator logs `R5900 Exception: Jump to unaligned address (PC: 0xcacacaca)` at emulator time 127.8897. It captured zero of the two requested frames.

The address matches CE's `debug_malloc(clear=FALSE)` fill pattern in `source/cseries/debug_memory.c:517`, so the evidence points to an indirect call through an uninitialized allocated field. The exact callsite is not identified: logs do not distinguish a later tick/action/game update from early work in the next `main_game_render`. Source inspection shows `game_set_game_variant` copies the local Slayer variant by value, so the local variant itself is not a dangling pointer. Treat this diagnosis as a hypothesis pending a focused trace.
