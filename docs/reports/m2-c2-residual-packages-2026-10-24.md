## 2. bots_sidewinder without bypass

- Why the pins were stale: `hcu_profile_package._pinned_non2d_evidence` reads `docs/reports/m2-c2-survival-non2d-consumers-2026-10-23.json`, whose `pins` hold the schema ELF `halo-local.elf` (`56cd9fb8...`, overwritten by a later closure build; now `1b7e191b...`) and `plans/7.hcu-remake-m2-m4.md` (`674ba2c9...`, now `e8c4df48...`; it was `6a5a728d...` during the earlier wrapped build, so the plan changed again since).
- Legitimate refresh path: `tools/audit_hcu_profile_non2d.py --profile survival`. It joins non-2D images to schema owners and requires `sha256(schema ELF) == diagnostic.schema_elf_sha256`, so the old `build/m2-c1-survival-dedup` diagnostic cannot be reused with the current ELF. The diagnostic itself must be regenerated by its producer.
- Real producer bug found and fixed: `tools/hcu_multiplayer_profiles.py build_one` raised `UnboundLocalError: presentation_provenance` when a `--report-failures` media-stage failure happened before `presentation_provenance` was assigned, masking the real error. Fix: initialise `presentation_provenance = None` after `memory = ...` (one line).
- Regenerated diagnostic: `python3 tools/hcu_multiplayer_profiles.py --profile survival-beavercreek --report-failures --verify-rebuild --output build/m2-c2-residual-packages/survival-diag` (225 s; log `survival-diag.log`).
