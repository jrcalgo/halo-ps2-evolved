## Method and limits

- `consumed` needs a named consumer and a member-exact parity fixture; family-level reports are recorded in `parity_evidence` but keep the member `blocked`.
- Lane parity rows (`docs/reports/m4-c4-parity-*.json`): a pass with member-exact scope and an existing test mentioning the field reclassifies a family-only row to `consumed`; a blocked result adds its notes to the gap reason; a fail sets gap_type `parity-failed` and is listed under Findings.
- `covered-by-member-rows` is assigned only when every overlapping live CE member has its own ledger row and the range bytes are covered numerically by those members plus declared pad/unused spans.
- `preserved-opaque` is assigned only when CE source contains zero `.name`/`->name` accesses. The search is textual, so macros and indirect access are not resolved.
- `retail_nonempty` is null for members the empty-field census does not audit (scalars, references).
- Host and synthetic evidence is not PCSX2 or retail-EE qualification.
