(excess 496,788 B). That check mixed owners, so it is replaced by the per-owner check above.

### Why no budget revision was implemented

The task allowed a revision only when measured margins of every affected owner prove it safe.
Candidate donors:

- `resident_gameplay` (cap 6,815,744 B): measured need is game-state reservation 3,424,256 B
  plus claims, at most 1,113,460 B over the 26 session rows. That leaves 2,278,028 B
  of slack. But the resident usage beyond the game-state reservation and claims is still
  unmeasured (C2.6 open), so the slack is not proven safe.
- `dynamic_simulation`, `system_kernel`, `frame_scratch`, `audio`, `checkpoint`:
  usage is unmeasured (C2.6), or the region is fixed by the DMA and renderer owners.
- `streaming`: after its validator scratch (590,224 B), staging slots (262,144 B) and the
  largest CTAG relocation staging (133,160 B), only 587,336 B remains. That is less than the
  1,126,032 B overflow.

So no region is proven safe to shrink. If the revision is later justified, the arithmetic is:
`resident_gameplay` 6,815,744 to 5,689,712 and `elf_static` 2,097,152 to 3,223,184, total
unchanged at 27,787,264. Gating `ai_debug` out of production (cross-lane, `source/ai`)
would cut the overflow to 578,404 B first.

## (b) The 27th profile

The archived set in `build/m2-c2-hcp2-primary/migration-report.json` has 27 profiles:
13 `bots-*`, 13 `survival-*` and `ui`. The session capacity matrix has 26 rows, one
`marine_shotgun` and one `mounted_gunner` per multiplayer map. The one archived profile with
no capacity row is `ui`, so it is the 27th.

- `ui` is measured from `docs/reports/m2-c2-profile-memory-audit-2026-10-23.json`
  with the same formulas: CE 789,137 B (aligned 789,152), BSP 2,288 B, render residual
  9,694,304 B, claims 98,936 B, CTAG relocation staging 10,040 B. It fits.
- `bots-*` and `survival-*` are archived too, but are not in the session matrix either.
  Their numbers come only from the profile-memory audit. `survival-bloodgulch` needs
  25,612,462 B of CE resident image, so it cannot fit a 10 MiB map/render backing at all. It is
  not represented in the table and remains a roster-residency case.

## (c) Renderer terms and simultaneous staging

Terms are taken from the headers, not typed in:

- `BSP_RENDER_WORKSPACE_BYTES` = 5,632 (`submodules/halo-ps2/engine/src/render/storage.h`).
- `HCU_RENDER_TEXTURE_RESERVED_BYTES` = 17 x 8,192 = 139,264 (`src/stream/hcu_render_telemetry.h`).
- `TC_PAGE_BYTES` = 8,192 (`submodules/halo-ps2/engine/src/render/texcache.h`).
- Staging: `STAGING_SLOTS` = 4 x `HCU_ASSET_PROFILE_MAX_MEDIA_CHUNK_BYTES` = 65,536
  (`src/stream/staging.h`, `src/stream/hcu_asset_profile.h`) = 262,144 B. This is charged to
  `streaming`, not to the map/render backing.

Per row: `render_residual_after_renderer_terms` = render residual - 5,632 - 139,264, and
`texture_cache_pages_available` = that residual div 8,192.

| Row | Render residual | After renderer terms | Texture pages | Simultaneous staging |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| `sidewinder_marine_shotgun_4p4bot` (smallest) | 1,578,064 | 1,433,168 | 174 | 393,908 |
| `beavercreek_marine_shotgun_4p4bot` | 2,916,608 | 2,771,712 | 338 | 392,356 |
| `longest_mounted_gunner_4p4bot` (largest) | 5,530,320 | 5,385,424 | 657 | 343,384 |
| `ui` | 9,694,304 | 9,549,408 | 1,165 | 272,184 |

The largest simultaneous staging is 395,304 B (`max_simultaneous_staging_bytes`), made of
the CTAG relocation staging (up to 133,160 B) plus the 262,144 B of slots.

Not measured: the renderer live-set index pages, target workload peaks and DMA lifetime at
a map boundary. Those are reported as `unmeasured` in `budget-audit.json`.

## Limits

- Attribution is by symbol name. A renamed or inlined buffer would be reclassified without notice.
  The owner rules are listed in `OWNER_RULES`, with their defining sources.
- Owned regions are assumed to include their static backing. That assumption is not measured.
- No live-workload peak is measured. The partition is a static capacity result.
